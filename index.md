## About Me

I am a **Professor** in the Division of Biostatistics & Health Data Science at the University of Minnesota School of Public Health, where I direct the **MPH Program in Public Health Data Science**.

My methodological research operates at the intersection of **causal inference** and **machine learning**, with emphasis on large, messy, and observational health datasets. Key applications include finding surrogate endpoints in clinical trials, risk prediction modeling for cardiovascular events using electronic health records (EHR), and analyzing continuous behavioral patterns from mobile health (mHealth) sensors.

I am also co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of **[Daynamica](http://www.daynamica.com)**, a University of Minnesota startup company providing smartphone-based data collection and spatial/behavioral analytics.
