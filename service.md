## Data and Safety Monitoring Boards (DSMB)

I serve (or have served) as an **unblinded statistician** on DSMBs for landmark randomized clinical trials coordinated at the University of Minnesota:

1. **PREVAIL I:** The first Phase 3 trial of an anti-Ebola vaccine, launched in Liberia in January 2015.
2. **LIFE-HIV:** Randomized evaluation of strategy for HIV management.
3. **TACTICAL-HIV:** Clinical strategy trial for HIV care delivery.
