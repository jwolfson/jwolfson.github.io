## Data and Safety Monitoring Boards (DSMB)

I serve (or have served) as an unblinded statistician on DSMBs for randomized trials coordinated at the University of Minnesota:
* **PREVAIL I:** The first Phase 3 trial of an anti-Ebola vaccine, launched in Liberia in 2015.
* **LIFE-HIV:** Randomized trial evaluating HIV treatment strategies.
* **TACTICAL-HIV:** Clinical strategy trial for HIV management.
